
TRASH TREK is an innovative mobile app that turns litter cleanup into an exciting game. By transforming a mundane task into a fun and rewarding experience, it's inspiring people to become eco-warriors.

TRASH TREK was awarded the prestigious INSPIRE MANAK AWARD 2023, recognizing its potential to revolutionize waste management. With a 10K INR grant from the government, Krishang, along with his talented sister, brought this vision to life.

The app uses augmented reality to identify litter, allowing players to collect virtual trash while cleaning up their surroundings. With features like Eco-Points, leaderboards, and educational content, TRASH TREK promotes healthy competition, environmental awareness, and community engagement.

A standout feature is the "Trashpedia," offers in-depth information about different types of waste and their impact on the environment. Using TypeScript and React Native craft the core components of TRASH TREK, including the engaging trivia quiz page, informative fun facts section, user-friendly home page, and comprehensive about us page. 

By incentivizing litter cleanup and fostering a sense of community, TRASH TREK is not just a game but a powerful tool for creating a cleaner, greener world.
 
Key Features:
* Augmented reality trash detection
* Eco-Points and rewards
* Leaderboards and community challenges
* Educational content (Trashpedia, quizzes)
* Real-world impact through community cleanups
